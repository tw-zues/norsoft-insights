4 Things Holding Leadership Back From AI

Sound familiar?

"I know AI matters but I haven't done anything with it yet"

Every trade show, every OEM conversation, every industry article. You know it's coming. But between running production, managing OEM deadlines, and keeping quality specs tight, figuring out where to start hasn't made the priority list.

It doesn't have to be

You don't need to become an AI expert. You don't need to evaluate tools or train your team on new software. AI connects to the systems you already have and reads from them without changing anything. Nothing changes about how you operate today.

The starting point is simple: what questions do you wish you could answer faster?

"I've tried ChatGPT but it doesn't touch my actual business"

Maybe you've used it to draft an email to a customer or summarize a spec sheet. That's fine. But it doesn't know your production runs, your scrap rates, your tooling schedules, or your customer quality requirements. It's working from the internet, not from your data.

The real version

When AI is connected to your actual business systems, the conversation changes completely. Instead of generic answers from the internet, you get real answers from your real data.

> What's our scrap rate by product line this month vs last quarter?
> Which jobs are behind schedule on the floor right now?
> Show me every open customer complaint tied to the rubber molding line

"I don't know how to make AI useful for my specific business"

The examples you see online are all tech companies or startups. Nobody is showing what AI looks like for a Tier 1 NVH supplier running precision molding and testing for automotive OEMs. So it stays theoretical.

What it looks like for SONUS

AI connected to your systems becomes a library of everything your company knows. Every test result, production run, quality report, customer spec, tooling record, and shipping log. Searchable in seconds.

Beyond searching, it starts working for you:

> Flag any production run where reject rate is trending above 3%
> Based on our last 50 NVH test reports, what's the typical pass rate by component type?
> Generate this week's production status report across all active jobs
> Which OEM customers haven't placed a new order in 6 months?

None of this requires new data. It's already in your systems, just locked behind logins and the one person who knows how to pull it.

"I'm not comfortable with AI touching our data"

You've heard the stories. Data leaking to AI companies. AI making things up. Sensitive OEM specs and production data ending up somewhere they shouldn't. The hesitation is rational. 70% of business owners who have adopted AI cite data privacy as their top concern.
