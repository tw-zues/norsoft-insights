Where your industry stands

Financial services talks about AI constantly. Most firms your size haven't actually connected it to anything.

The big banks have AI teams. The fintechs were born on it. But equipment finance companies in the 50-200 employee range are in a different spot. The data lives in loan management systems, credit platforms, and spreadsheets that were set up years ago. AI sounds great in theory. In practice, nobody has shown you what it actually does for a direct lender in St. Cloud.

Three things we hear from companies like yours

The gap between knowing and doing

1

"I know AI matters but I'm not sure where to start safely"

Your loan data, customer financials, credit decisions, and payment histories are sensitive. You can't just feed them into ChatGPT and hope for the best. The risk of getting it wrong is real, especially in a regulated space. So the default has been to wait. The problem is waiting has its own cost.
"Everyone is talking about AI and how it is going to replace everyone's jobs and how you have to adopt it. There seems to be a lot of BS hype and ideas but I have seen very few real uses that are changing how people do business." — Business owner, 2025
2

"We use ChatGPT for emails. That's about it."

Maybe someone on the team uses it to draft follow-up emails or summarize a credit memo. That's fine. But it doesn't know your portfolio, your delinquency rates, your dealer network, or which accounts are coming up for renewal. It's working from the internet, not from your data. That's like asking someone who's never seen your book to tell you how your portfolio is performing.
3

"Getting a real answer from our own systems takes longer than it should"

You want to know which ag accounts are falling behind. Or how this quarter's deal volume compares to last year. Or which vendor relationships are actually producing. That answer is split between three or four different systems and a spreadsheet someone maintains on the side. By the time it comes together, the meeting already happened.
What AI actually looks like for an equipment finance company

You type a question. You get a real answer from your real data.

No new software to learn. No ripping out what you already use. AI connects directly to what's already running and you ask it questions in plain English.

Here are the kinds of questions Northland Capital could ask on day one:

> Which accounts are more than 30 days past due right now?
> What's our average deal size this quarter vs. same quarter last year?
> Which vendors are generating the most new deal volume this year?
> Show me all ag loans with balloon payments coming due in the next 90 days
> What's our approval-to-funding ratio by loan officer this month?
> Which customers have paid off their loan and might be ready for new equipment?

None of this requires new data. It's already there, just locked behind logins and the one person who knows how to pull it.

Why it's safe

Your data stays exactly where it is

The first question everyone in financial services asks is "what about our customer data?" Fair question. Here's the short answer: it never leaves your building.

🔒

Read-only by default

AI can look at your data but can't touch it. No accidental changes to loan records, payment histories, or credit files.

🏠

Data stays on your servers

Queries run locally. Customer financials, loan terms, and deal data never leave your environment. Not for training, not for anything.

📑

Full audit trail

Every question asked and every answer returned is logged. Important for compliance. You see exactly what was accessed and when.

🔌

Unplug anytime

If it's not a fit, we disconnect it and you're out nothing. Your systems go back to exactly how they were.
