Three things we hear from owners like you

The gap between knowing and doing

1

"I know AI matters but I haven't done anything with it yet"

Every conference, every LinkedIn post, every trade article mentions AI. You know it's coming for your industry. But between running jobs, managing subs, and keeping the backlog healthy, figuring out where to start hasn't made the priority list.
"Feels like every day there's another AI ad, another person trying to sell some tool. But are other small business owners actually using AI in a way that makes a real difference?" — GC owner, 2025
2

"I've tried ChatGPT but it doesn't touch my actual business"

Maybe you've used it to draft an RFI response or summarize a spec document. That's fine. But it doesn't know your jobs, your margins, your subs, or your change orders. It's working from the internet, not from your data. That's like asking someone who's never been on your jobsite to tell you how the project is going.
"I keep trying to use it, but it's consistently a waste of time outside of extremely basic tasks." — Business owner, 2025
3

"I can't get a straight answer from my own systems without calling someone"

You want to know which jobs are over budget this month. That answer is split between your estimating software, your PM tool, and your accounting system. By the time someone puts it together, the job is further along and the number already changed. The person who knows how everything works is the bottleneck, and if they leave, a lot of knowledge walks out the door with them.
What AI actually looks like for a GC your size

You type a question. You get a real answer from your real data.

No new software to learn. No ripping out what you already use. No training your team on another dashboard. AI connects to the systems you already run your business on, and you ask it questions in plain English.

Here are the kinds of questions a company like Harmon Construction could ask on day one:

> Which jobs went over budget last quarter?
> What's our margin on the Overland Park senior living project?
> Which subs have the most open change orders right now?
> Show me every project where we're more than 10% over the original estimate
> What's our average close rate on healthcare bids vs commercial?
> Which customers haven't started a new project with us in 18 months?

These answers already exist in your systems. They're just trapped behind logins, reports, and the one person who knows how to pull them.

Why it's safe

Your data stays exactly where it is

The first question every owner asks is "what if it changes something?" Fair question. Here's the short answer: it can't.

🔒

Read-only by default

AI can look at your data but can't touch it. No accidental deletes, no rogue updates, no surprises at 2am.

🏠

Data stays on your servers

Nothing gets uploaded to the cloud for AI training. Your financials, your bids, your client data stays in your building.

📑

Full audit trail

Every question asked and every answer returned is logged. You see exactly what AI accessed and when.

🔌

Unplug anytime

If you hate it, we disconnect it and you're out nothing. Your systems go back to exactly how they were.
