4 Things Holding Leadership Back From AI

Sound familiar?

"I know AI matters but I haven't done anything with it yet"

Every conference, every LinkedIn post, every trade article. You know it's coming. But between running jobs, managing subs, and keeping the backlog healthy, figuring out where to start hasn't made the priority list.

It doesn't have to be

You don't need to become an AI expert. You don't need to evaluate tools or train your team on new software. AI connects to the systems you already have and reads from them without changing anything. Nothing changes about how you operate today.

The starting point is simple: what questions do you wish you could answer faster?

"I've tried ChatGPT but it doesn't touch my actual business"

Maybe you've used it to draft an RFI response or summarize a spec document. That's fine. But it doesn't know your jobs, your margins, your subs, or your change orders. It's working from the internet, not from your data.

The real version

When AI is connected to your actual business systems, the conversation changes completely. Instead of generic answers from the internet, you get real answers from your real data.

> Which jobs went over budget last quarter?
> What's our margin on the Overland Park senior living project?
> Which subs have the most open change orders right now?

"I don't know how to make AI useful for my specific business"

The examples you see online are all tech companies or startups. Nobody is showing what AI looks like for a GC running healthcare and senior living projects in the midwest. So it stays theoretical.

What it looks like for Harmon

AI connected to your systems becomes a library of everything your company knows. Every bid, contract, change order, invoice, inspection report, and sub agreement. Searchable in seconds.

Beyond searching, it starts working for you:

> Flag any project trending more than 10% over the original estimate
> Based on our last 20 healthcare projects, what should this bid look like?
> Generate this week's project status report across all active jobs
> Which customers haven't started a new project with us in 18 months?

None of this requires new data. It's already in your systems, just locked behind logins and the one person who knows how to pull it.

"I'm not comfortable with AI touching our data"

You've heard the stories. Data leaking to AI companies. AI making things up. Sensitive financials ending up somewhere they shouldn't. The hesitation is rational. 70% of business owners who have adopted AI cite data privacy as their top concern.
